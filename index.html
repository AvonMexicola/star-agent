Your next giant leap.

Click the view to capture your mouse. Look around, pick a direction, and go. Atmospheric flight cruises at 250 m/s, with a modest 400 m/s boost. Speed rises smoothly to 3 km/s in space, or 9 km/s boosted. Ground and station approaches reduce these limits. Near the star, stellar cruise allows longer approaches; Space + Shift thrusts away from the star. Shield temperature rises with radiation and damages the hull if cooling cannot keep up. The drive stops 500,000 km above the photosphere. Manual flight may enter the danger zone; below 20,000 km the ship is destroyed immediately. Press V for inertial flight: momentum and rotation persist until countered or braked with X.

In controls, use the right stick or D-pad ↑ / ↓ to scroll. Destinations, seed entry and sound use the mouse or keyboard.

After landing, press F to stand. Walk aft to the rear hatch and press F to open it and lower the ramp. The orange cargo container on the starboard cabin wall opens with F; take or stow supplies in your backpack. Four cockpit MFDs show live flight, navigation, systems and cargo data. Walk outside, explore, then return up the ramp and approach the pilot chair. F seats you; L launches.

M opens the system map. Select a world and engage the drive; J also engages a selected route. The drive aligns, spools for 3 seconds and brakes above the destination. X aborts with controlled braking. Maximum speed is 0.9c; short routes reach a lower peak. Leave Aeon’s 100 km, Selene’s 20 km or Pyre’s 40 km exclusion zone and ensure a clear route first. Opening the map pauses flight. Aeon, Selene and Pyre have continuous surfaces to explore. Quick transit is an optional teleport; manual flight crosses the atmosphere without scene changes. Desktop keyboard and mouse recommended.

At Aeon Orbital, fly through the open hangar doors and brake over the central pad. L docks. Walk down your ship’s ramp to explore the deck, then return to the chair. L lifts off gently; S reverses out through the doors.

Shift + click a destination to set a flight course. Follow the bearing with your normal flight controls; climb when your destination is beyond the horizon. A plain click uses optional quick transit.

Pyre is the hot inner planet, 10 M km from the star and tidally locked: one face bakes at 400 °C, the other glows with lava fields. Click Pyre to arrive 60 km above the dusk terminator, or select it on the system map and engage the drive. The thin CO₂ air allows landing (L) and walking (F); the HUD shows hull temperature on the day side.

Selene is Aeon’s airless, cratered moon. Click Selene for a descent approach, or Shift + click to set a continuous-flight course. L lands, F leaves the chair. Walk aft, open the hatch with F, and walk down the ramp. Space jumps in lunar gravity. Return to the chair, F sits, and L launches. O returns to Aeon orbit.

Opening a seed starts a new flight. Share the resulting URL: the same seed and generator version produce the same planet for everyone.
