In controls, use the right stick or D-pad ↑ / ↓ to scroll. Destinations, seed entry and sound use the mouse or keyboard.

After landing, press F to stand. Walk aft to the rear hatch and press F to open it and lower the ramp. The orange cargo container on the starboard cabin wall opens with F; take or stow supplies in your backpack. Four cockpit MFDs show live flight, navigation, systems and cargo data. Walk outside, explore, then return up the ramp and approach the pilot chair. F seats you; L launches.

One continuous planet at ¼ Earth radius. Quick transit is an optional teleport; manual flight crosses the atmosphere without scene changes. Desktop keyboard and mouse recommended.

At Aeon Orbital, fly through the open hangar doors and brake over the central pad. L docks. Walk down your ship’s ramp to explore the deck, then return to the chair. L lifts off gently; S reverses out through the doors.

Shift + click a destination to set a flight course. Follow the bearing with your normal flight controls; climb when your destination is beyond the horizon. A plain click uses optional quick transit.

Selene is Aeon’s airless, cratered moon. Click Selene for a descent approach, or Shift + click to set a continuous-flight course. L lands, F leaves the chair. Walk aft, open the hatch with F, and walk down the ramp. Space jumps in lunar gravity. Return to the chair, F sits, and L launches. O returns to Aeon orbit.

Opening a seed starts a new flight. Share the resulting URL: the same seed and generator version produce the same planet for everyone.
