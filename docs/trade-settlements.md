# Trade settlements

The solo star system has four world-owned settlements assembled from the existing
construction kit. Open **Map → a world → Locations**, or use the **Trade settlements**
filter in Signals. Select the exchange to track its flight beacon. The normal
relativistic route stops 20 km above the surface; descend and land normally.

| World | Settlement | Speciality |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aeon | Greenbank Supply | Aggregate and construction stock |
| Selene | Stillwater Exchange | Water ice and mineral freight |
| Pyre | Ember Works | Metal stock and conductors |
| Miasma | Verdigris Prospect | Copper ore |

Each settlement has one designated **48 × 72 m large landing pad**, three enclosed
kit buildings, storage racks, working-area lights and power props. The pads share
flush door thresholds. Their 8 m piers sit on surveyed canonical terrain. Roofs,
windows, doors and floors use the construction system's existing geometry and
collision. Buildings and facility props belong to the settlement; they are not
player inventory or extra player claims. Doors are held open for public access.
No resident NPC population or life-support simulation is included.

Land the selected ship within the pad, leave through its actual cabin/ramp route,
and walk through the rear exchange's open doorway. **F / controller X** at the
inventory-terminal prop opens **Cargo & trade**. Buy stock into the ship's real
SBU grid, or choose **Cargo** to sell a shipment. Each exchange has independent,
finite stock and dynamic quotes. Changes save with solo cargo; reloading does not
restock an exhausted warehouse. Prices and stock create different trade routes.

With fresh stock, try ice from Stillwater to Ember Works, metal stock from Ember
Works to Verdigris, or copper from Verdigris to Stillwater. Check the current
quote before buying: previous trades change the margin and available quantity.

Controller entry uses **Menu → Map**, D-pad/A to select a world and settlement,
then B to return. Land/launch with Y; use X for the pilot, hatch and exchange.
The trade dialog uses the shared D-pad/A/B controls. Existing keyboard and touch
map/trade buttons remain available.

For local inspection, **F2 / Dev → Ship & location** lists each named exchange.
It starts the chosen ship 65 m above the pad. This is an explicit development
approach; those practice inventories reset on a new test launch. Ordinary solo
play uses the browser's persistent cargo save.

This content is currently solo only. Shared multiplayer world collision and
server-owned settlement markets require a separate integration; solo exchanges
and beacons disappear when connected. Player-built shared commerce is a separate
feature. No production deployment is implied by the local checkpoint.
