# Deer rig and gait repair

Cees supplied the newest downloaded walking deer and asks for a more elegant rig.
Inspect the actual mesh, skeleton, weights and animation before identifying a
cause. Preserve the original source. Correct demonstrated joint/weight defects;
avoid skeleton surgery when gait keys and contact are the actual problem.

Retain the long-legged antlered silhouette and authored albedo. Produce a graceful,
grounded walk with controlled stride width, coherent hock/knee bends, stable hoof
support and restrained body/head motion. Keep a metre-scale Y-up, -Z-forward GLB,
an editable Blender rig and reproducible source-to-export script. Record before
and after posed evidence plus dense animation/contact validation and limitations.

This request covers rig/walk repair and a reviewable export. Deer biome, spawning,
combat, drops and death animation are not yet specified or implemented.
