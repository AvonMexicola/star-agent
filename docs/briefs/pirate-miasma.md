vacuum bases, with no tents or fabric shelters. Hush Exchange retains its Selene
location and stock identity. Veil Exchange adds a second independent solo market
in Miasma's Vitriol Basin, surveyed against canonical terrain and connected to
its outer landing pad by a bounded route around rock relief.

Both enclosed trade habitats use solid roofs, sealed wall/window kit modules and
an eight-metre vestibule with two actual interlocked doors. Existing BuildSystem
leaf animation and swept collision protect entrants; no atmosphere or pressure
simulation is claimed. Crimson structural finishes and rigid faction/airlock
prints consume the parent's appearance API. Original tower and all four supplied
Crimson props are reused without new geometry or texture derivatives.

Each site keeps independent session discovery, tower isolation and finite market
stock. Additive market version2 seeds Veil once while preserving all Hush stock.

burst rest remain unchanged. No separate ground enemy modules are consumed.
