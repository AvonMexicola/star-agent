# Pirate vacuum bases

Development candidate: Hush Exchange on Selene and Veil Exchange on Miasma.
Both are solo Crimson Pact trade compounds with rigid enclosed habitats, sealed
kit roofs and a two-door entrance. The doors interlock mechanically; the game
does not simulate pressure or breathable habitat air. Exterior work yards use
the supplied generator, workbench, crates and tripod lights. There are no tents.

Use the local developer launcher's Hush or Veil entry to approach its outer
landing apron. In normal play the map shows an unidentified trade signal until
you approach within 1,000 metres. Discovery and tower isolation are independent
for each site and last for the session. Both markets keep independent finite
stock in the existing solo trade save; loading an older Hush save adds Veil once
and preserves Hush's current stock.

1. Lower landing gear and land on the marked outer apron. On a controller, hold
   LB + RB and press D-pad Down for gear, then Y to land.
2. Leave the chair with X, physically walk through the ship hatch and down its
   ramp. Follow the amber ground-approach marker around the raised apron to the
   compound's supported access ramp.
3. Reach the tower isolator beside the cylindrical tower. Press F or X to open
   it, then select **Isolate tower and unlock exchange** (A confirms, B closes).
4. Approach the rigid trade habitat. Face a door and press F or X to open it.
   The opposite door closes before the selected door opens. Step clear if a
   closing leaf pauses against you. Touch controls show the same door action.
5. Enter through both doors and use the terminal to buy supplies, view cargo
   or sell cargo. The actual outer-apron ship is the cargo destination. Close
   the dialog with B, Escape or its visible Resume control, then use both doors
   to leave.

The turret warns aircraft within 300 metres and starts its attack cycle inside
180 metres. Its five-second warning precedes a visible 1.2-second charge. A
normal burst is three 18-damage shots, followed by five seconds of rest. Every
supported ship survives an individual hit and a normal burst at full health.
Move beyond 220 metres to end engagement. Parked ships with a pilot aboard
remain targets; walking outside or driving a ground rover does not target the
parked ship. Ground access and the outer landing apron remain outside this
ship-defense route. Isolating a tower disables it for that visit.

This encounter uses the existing ship-damage system and a reachable trader. It
does not add ground NPC enemies or consume unfinished pirate-camp work. See the
[production record](qa/pirate-miasma/README.md) for actual validation and limits.
