# Current candidate review status

The independent Claude Opus review of the initial model scored **2.4/5** and did not approve it. Its findings and first captures remain in `first-visual-review.md` and `first-*.png`. The user subsequently identified ferry-like glazing, pressure gaps and obstructed galley circulation; those criticisms were valid.

Real mesh contact shading supplements authored PBR maps. Export tests caught and corrected a material/vertex-colour fallback before final review.
