## Ranked closures

1. **Remove the new floor lattice and check small-face material filtering.** Compare `desktop-cabin.png` with Art11: the new floor is crossed by bright white/amber lines at dense cell boundaries, rather than a restrained graphite walking surface. This is a concrete appearance blocker. The AO subdivision may remain, but it should not become a visible material seam at every cell. Check contiguous floor UVs or a safely inset/material-specific sampling region before changing geometry or adding lighting. Also inspect the new cushion patches and small welt faces under the same actual minification conditions. Preserve the floor boundary, support plane and local AO measurements.

   A bounded read-only GLB probe confirms all **3,600 upward-facing floor vertices have normal `[0,1,0]`** and the same decoded Y **1.400034805 m**. The floor is flat; no bright physical grid was authored in this mesh. Its top faces repeatedly use almost the full graphite tile, UV **U .25390625–.49609375 / V .00390625–.49609375**, only four source pixels from neighboring swatches. Raw tile corners are approximately RGB **38–39 / 50–51 / 56**, while the adjacent ceramic and metal are much brighter. The sampler uses linear mipmapped minification (`9987`). **Atlas bleed during minification is a strong inference, not a GPU-isolated diagnosis.** `probe-floor.py` and `floor-atlas-diagnostic.json` retain the exact measurements; receipt SHA **`06efbcbda69d81a6a57766b21eb2633a3aaa90d2b62de13e2340b97604f10191`**. A corrective native result must close the appearance, not merely pass a raw swatch sample.

2. **Finish the few remaining primary-form transitions.** Preserve the improved fin roots, narrower crown, thermal waist and drive housings. In `desktop-side.png` and `desktop-exterior.png`, the large cowl side/crown still carries a fairly simple capsule profile, while the fore shoulder, cockpit roof and low prow end as separate stacked masses. Refine the relationship of those slopes/termination points so the forward pressure cell and side protection read as one deliberate load-carrying shell. Adding more small service plates will not change this silhouette judgment. Keep the occupied pressure cell, eye, clear bay and mechanism envelopes authoritative; do not put structure across the pilot view.

3. **Close the material/contact appearance after the mapping issue is isolated.** Rounded pad geometry is a useful improvement and should stay. The current sage pads still have bright, hard perimeter/corner bands that compete with the intended compliant surface; verify the actual runtime sampling and response before another tiny noise adjustment. The bay ceiling hotspot improvement should also stay. Tire/platform contact is still barely readable in the native bay close view, and furniture bases need clearer local depth without a noisy floor or broad painted dark patches. Existing submillimetre contact measurements do not support moving the rover or furniture merely to make a shadow: solve the visible response with the physical surfaces in place.

4. **Retain the closed Art11 findings.** Nozzle recesses, useful fore/waist/aft hierarchy, desktop/portrait MFD access and nominal mechanism motion remain successful. There is no new central strut or critical framing crop in this review. The recorded eye error is about **0.000000384 m** at `[0,2.95,-8.4]`, 60° FOV, and all 12 recorded chrome-overlap lists are empty. This does not certify the main-game camera or input route.
