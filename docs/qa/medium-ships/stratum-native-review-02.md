## Bounded remaining priorities

1. **Primary shape and fitted shoulder transitions.** Compare [exterior](/tmp/star-agent-stratum-native-art02/stratum-studio-Stratum-nat-271d7-and-real-mechanism-controls/01-exterior.png), [side](/tmp/star-agent-stratum-native-art02/stratum-studio-Stratum-nat-271d7-and-real-mechanism-controls/02-side.png) and [top](/tmp/star-agent-stratum-native-art02/stratum-studio-Stratum-nat-271d7-and-real-mechanism-controls/03-top.png). Give the existing shoulder caps a more deliberate forward tool-root transition, service waist and aft drive collar. Integrate the extraction-boom roots into the load-bearing shoulder with a few substantial shaped transitions; repeated small top ribs cannot supply that structure. Refine the center roof's broad cap into a clearly seated pressure/service assembly, retaining the existing clear pilot glass, cabin volume and overall envelope. This needs primary geometry work, not another coating-only pass.

2. **Broad-surface contact and manufactured finish.** The new panels are an improvement, but the large white caps and dark upper cell still have elementary faceted responses. Use fitted bevel/gasket depth, plausible overlapping panel relationships and restrained roughness separation at those existing seams. Keep the current tool/nozzle material improvement. Inspect the regular diagonal streak read in roof/ceiling close views and in movement; the images establish its appearance, not whether its cause is geometry, UVs or textures. Correct the actual cause rather than masking it with brighter lighting or many small details. Improve the contact of existing cabin plinths, bin lids and wall-mounted fittings without reducing the aisle.

3. **Finish evidence from the actual useful views.** Preserve these clean desktop/phone runs. Add a closer underside sequence through both complete gear directions and visible yaw plus elevation extremes, returning to neutral. The current whole ship occupies a small central area in the gear canvas. Demonstrate phone access to each outer MFD at the same canonical eye using the intended look control; do not move the pilot or distort the lens solely to fit four screens into portrait. Then evaluate the actual game cabin, mining action and physical boarding route separately.

## Disposition and limits

**Changes required for art acceptance.** Art02 is a substantially improved development checkpoint with a clean supplied studio run. Preserve its clear cockpit, functional ramp, fitted storage and lower-gloss working hardware while improving the remaining primary forms and broad surface construction.

This review establishes no full-game flight, mining, physical traversal, persistence, online authority, controller-hardware, long-run resource or FPS acceptance. Native phone studio capture is distinct from a complete touch gameplay journey. Cees retains final product/release authority. The earlier failed review and this assessment should both remain in the iteration history.
