## Closure and remaining limits

I read the Art03 failed review and directly compared its original desktop exterior, side, top, cabin and bores views with the corresponding Art04 originals.

1. **The principal silhouette blocker is closed in these views.** The old slender straight arms with three repeated top blocks are replaced by a deep tapered casing with a deliberate root and head transition. The formerly dominant high shoulder and roof masses now step into the rest of the body. This is visible in the same whole-ship views and remains coherent through the yaw/elevation sequence; it is not a texture-only improvement.
2. **The outlined-panel problem is sufficiently reduced for this scoped review.** Major structural boundaries remain readable while secondary joints are narrower. The broad skins still need polish to reach a higher finish score, but they no longer obscure the primary assembly hierarchy. Good dark bores and restrained rims are retained.
3. **Cabin contact is improved, with a residual lighting limitation.** New fitted end panels and the tighter plinth/lid/wall junctions give the bins physical depth. The large regular dark contact patches from Art03 are reduced. The cabin remains more evenly lit and less rich than the exterior; faint ceiling variation is recorded rather than declared completely eliminated. The static originals do not identify each remaining trace's map/normal/shadow origin.
4. **Mechanism and display evidence stays sufficient.** Both recordings expose nominal ramp and gear return cycles. Capture receipts record port/starboard yaw with opposite signs across ±0.20 rad and shared pitch from −0.12 to +0.14 rad, then exact neutral. Buttons name the next requested action, so a “Deploy ramp” label while the ramp is still closing is not treated as a false instantaneous state.
