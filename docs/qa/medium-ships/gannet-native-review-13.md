## Closed findings and remaining refinements

1. **Art12's floor and pad blockers are closed in the actual native images.** The floor's bright repeated lattice and the metallic-looking pad outlines are absent in `desktop-cabin.png`. The same improvement extends to small cabinet and graphite-panel edges. The author attributes this to continuous/safely inset UV sampling and textile-family welt assignment; the independently checked unchanged embedded image bytes are consistent with a mapping/material-assignment correction. This report does not treat the author's finite filtering probe as a substitute for the inspected render.
2. **The requested primary-form closure is sufficient.** `desktop-exterior.png`, `desktop-side.png` and both top views show longer connected fore transitions and less dominant cowl crowns. The recognizable rear bay, drive ends, fin roots and thermal waist remain. Further broad-face sophistication could improve the design, but I do not identify another silhouette blocker in this captured candidate.
3. **Highest remaining visual refinement: bay contact and local depth.** In `desktop-bay-burrow.png`, the tire/platform junctions and underside of the rover have weak local shadow definition. A future physically grounded contact response should preserve the actual support geometry and avoid broad painted dark patches. The remaining pale ceiling and relatively uniform deck also limit depth. No new lighting change is required to make this scoped review pass, and none was made during review.
4. **Second refinement: broad manufactured finish.** The large ivory cowl/fin faces and near cabinet fronts are still quite plain compared with their bevels and inset edges. Sparse, clearly sized service finish or coating variation could improve their material identity. Random noise, extra brightness or a return to visible atlas seams would be regressions. This is retained quality headroom, not an unreported acceptance blocker.
5. **Retain the successful forward view and functional hierarchy.** Do not fill the clear windshield with a central brace or obscure the actual MFD faces. The current eye and portrait look access are successful in this studio scope. The raw loaded-cargo measurement, main-game lighting and complete input journeys require their separate records.
