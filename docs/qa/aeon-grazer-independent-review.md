## Concrete findings

1. **Final pose correction succeeds within these views.** The neck bends down toward the forepaws rather than carrying the head above the torso. The barrel chest, dorsal ridges and belly volume remain recognizable; the body has not been flattened to achieve support. Rear oblique shows asymmetrical limb placement rather than a rigid symmetric stand. No obvious torn seam, disconnected joint or inverted limb is visible.
2. **Some resting ambiguity remains, without a blocking final-pose defect.** The eyes remain open and the tail tip curves upward, but the lowered head and low body outweigh those cues. The posture could also serve a sleeping/resting animal; animation context must distinguish death in play. No unsupported claim of expressive facial death animation or ragdoll physics is made.
3. **Flat support is plausible, not universally proved.** Belly and folded limbs visually settle against the studio floor without gross floating or broad burial. Manifest reports121 sampled death phases with worst ground minimum−3.059mm and reimport bounds error6.199µm. I read these measurements but did not independently repeat them. Head/forepaw overlaps should still be checked through the transition, and these numbers do not establish uneven terrain contact.
4. **Living motion remains outside this inspection.** Source views substantiate appearance and connected anatomy, not gait quality. Preserving the broad source body does not automatically approve its supplied walk at a future gameplay speed. No biome rendering, AI behavior, peacefulness, controller/touch journey or FPS claim is established here.
