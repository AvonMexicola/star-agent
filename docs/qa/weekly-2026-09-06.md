# Star Agent — weekly quality pass, 2026-09-06 (BASELINE)

Reviewer: Opus (senior game art/UI). Branch: integration tree at `scratchpad/wt-vf` (HEAD `77f678c`).
Build: `vite build` green, 46 modules, 794 kB JS. **Zero console errors and zero warnings** across three full
Playwright runs (orbit tour, intro/boarding, full-render-scale re-capture).

Method: headless Chromium + SwiftShader, 1600×900, `?intro=0&seed=7291` (and `?intro=1` for the opening).
**SwiftShader dropped the app's automatic render scale to 0.55**, so the first pass is upscaled from 880×495.
Every damning surface viewpoint was therefore re-captured with `setRenderScale(1)` (`r1-*.png`) — the material
flatness, the LOD banding and the blown snow are all still there at full resolution, so they are real, not
resolution artefacts. **fps (0–9) is not hardware-representative and is excluded from scoring.** Draw calls and
triangles are.

Screenshots: `scratchpad/qa/baseline/*.png` (01–13 at the automatic scale, `r1-01`…`r1-07` at scale 1.0).

**Overall score: 2.6 / 5.** The merge bar in QUALITY.md §2 is 4.0 with no item below 3. Nine of the thirteen
viewpoints score below 3.0, and two (Selene surface, polar ice) are below 2.0. Cees's complaint is confirmed and
under-stated: the map blobs are the *smallest* of the whitebox problems.

## 3. Ranked top-10 defects

Impact-ordered. Whiteboxy candidates first.

**1 — Selene's surface is an untextured plane. `r1-07-moon.png`. Owner: Astra + Blender script.**
The worst frame in the build: flat beige, no craters, no rocks, no relief. Two causes, both fixable.
(a) `src/moon-terrain.js` builds `budget = 8` patches/frame and `get ready()` returns true at `maxLevel >= 2`, so
the QA gate and the loading screen both pass on a six-quad blob — measured `lod: 3` at 180 m altitude.
(b) `moonSurface()` in `src/moon-world.js` has no metre-scale relief to find even at max LOD.
*Fix:* raise the build budget to ~64/frame while `moonAltitude < 5 km` and pre-warm the patch column along
`MOON_LANDING_DIRECTION` during the transit; require `maxLevel >= 12` in `ready` below 2 km; add two octaves at
~30 m and ~8 m wavelength (±1.5 m, ±0.3 m) plus a regolith normal; scatter boulder props (`boulder-cracked`,
`boulder-layered` already exist) and small fresh-crater rims near the landing site.

**2 — Snow has no material at all. `05-polar.png`, `r1-04-highlands.png`. Owner: Astra.**
`src/surface-materials.js` `configureTerrainMaterial`: the snow branch is
`detail.rgb = mix(detail.rgb, diffuseColor.rgb*(.88+soil.a*.2), snow)` — it throws away all four ground layers and
leaves flat vertex colour. Combined with `DirectionalLight(0xfff1dc, 3.4)` (`src/lighting.js:6`) against a ~1.0
albedo, ACES at exposure 1.08 clips ~70 % of the polar frame to paper white.
*Fix:* add a fifth layer to `createGroundTextures()` (wind sastrugi ridging, ice crust, grain sparkle), route snow
through it instead of bypassing, drop snow linear albedo to ~0.62, keep `detailNormal` active on snow, and tint
snow shadow toward the sky colour instead of neutral grey.

**3 — The "coast" QA viewpoint has no coast. `r1-02-coast.png`. Owner: Astra.**
`state.biome` reads COASTLAND and there is no water, beach or shoreline in frame — a bare olive plane to a stepped
horizon, the exact "flat plane view" §1 forbids. Vegetation there is 140 trees, **all** far-impostor
(`treeLods:[0,0,132]`), and 213 rocks that read as white polystyrene beads with no contact shadow.
*Fix:* re-pick `findDestinations().coast` in `src/world.js` to sit ≤ 300 m from the shoreline and set the arrival
heading (`src/main.js:83`) to face the sea; add a wet-sand shoreline band, dune grass and drift debris to the
coastal scatter; darken and desaturate the rock instance albedo and add a small AO decal under each.

**4 — The forest is three different forests with a hard edge. `r1-03-forest.png`. Owner: Astra.**
Near LOD0 foliage renders near-black (it casts *and* receives shadow via `customDepthMaterial` with no leaf
transmission), LOD1 is mid olive, LOD2 impostors are pale sage and further lifted by
`diffuseColor.rgb *= mix(.5,.85,vMapUv.y)` (`src/vegetation.js:76`); `alphaTest` also differs .32 / .27 / .2. All
41 k trees then stop dead at ~1400 m (`src/tree-lod.js` `TREE_RADIUS 1480`, far band `[1200,1400]`).
*Fix:* calibrate all three materials to one measured mean albedo; add wrap/transmission lighting to the LOD0 leaf
shader so near canopy isn't a silhouette; unify `alphaTest`; and paint a forest-tint term into the land shader
from 1.4 km out so the cutoff has nothing to reveal.

**5 — The cockpit is a whitebox. `13-cockpit.png`. Owner: Blender script + Astra.**
From the pilot seat: three flat grey canopy struts and four floating MFD quads. No console, coaming, glareshield,
side panels, seat, stick or ship nose — nothing says you are inside a vehicle. The MFD canvas in `src/ship-mfd.js`
is also under-resolved, so the (excellent) page layouts render as mush at 1600×900.
*Fix:* hard-surface pass on `nomad.glb` for a forward console with bezels around the four MFDs, a glareshield, two
side consoles, throttle and stick, and a seat rim in the lower frame; raise the MFD canvas to 1024² and re-render
with the token type stack.

**6 — Station hull is one grey. `06-station.png`. Owner: Blender script.**
`blender/build_station.py:70-71` — `Hull (0.78,0.78,0.78)` and `HullPanel (0.62,0.64,0.66)`, no panel-line/AO/wear
texture, and `weatherShip()` (`src/surface-materials.js`) is applied only to the Nomad (`src/main.js:47`), never
to the station. Faction language calls for mint accents; the station shows two red nav pips and no mint.
*Fix:* bake a panel-line + AO + edge-wear atlas in the build script, split `Hull` into three tonal variants,
add `MintPaint` stripes at the hangar mouth and module ends and lit `Window` rows along the hab spine, then run a
`weatherStation()` pass on load.

**7 — The system map does not map. `08-map-desktop.png`, `09-map-phone.png`. Owner: Astra.**
The defect that started QUALITY.md, unchanged. Three CSS circles (27 / 71 / 33 px) in `src/system-map.css`; no
ship marker; `.map-route-line` is a static rotated 1 px `div` at fixed `left:61%/top:31%`; neither body sits on
either `.map-orbit-*` ellipse; no scale bar; the station is absent; DISTANCE / ETA / APPROACH are "—" on open
although every value is already computable from `nav.travelRoute()`. On phone the dialog clips its own footer.
*Fix:* replace the CSS chart with inline SVG driven by `travel-model.js` — Aeon on a drawn orbit arc, Selene on a
real circular orbit around Aeon, the star at true bearing, a ship marker with a heading tick, the selected route
as an arc labelled with live km and ETA, a log-scale distance bar, and the station as a marker in Aeon orbit.
Populate the `dl` on open. Give the phone layout `overflow-y:auto` on the dialog body with the footer pinned.

**8 — Hangars and cabins are not lit by their lights. `10-hangar-t10.png`, `12-ramp.png`. Owner: Astra.**
§1 is explicit: "Hangars are lit by their lights." `src/station.js:192-196` adds an `AmbientLight` at intensity
**0** and two PointLights that do not cast shadows; the `HangarLight` / `Window` emissives (strength 4 and 3,
doubled on load) glow but spill nothing. The cabin has one PointLight (`src/ship-walkable.js:225`) and its mint
ceiling strips do not touch the floor. Nothing in either space has a contact shadow — the astronaut floats.
*Fix:* 4–6 shadow-casting lights down the bay plus a warm bounce fill sized to the deck; one shadow-casting cabin
light plus vertex-baked AO in the cabin geometry; a cheap blob contact shadow under the character.

**9 — Two §5 budget failures: orbit draw calls and the map modal. Owner: Astra.**
Orbit is **477** draw calls against a ≤ 300 budget, because `src/planet.js` issues a land mesh *and* a water mesh
for every one of the 202 visible patches. And the system map renders the scene behind it at full cost —
201 calls / 128 002 triangles with the dialog open, identical to the frame behind it, against a "skipped or
≤ 25 %" budget.
*Fix:* skip the water mesh on patches whose max terrain height is above sea level (the worker already knows);
merge sibling patches at level ≤ 4 into one geometry; and gate the render loop on `document.querySelector('dialog[open]')`
— skip the frame, or drop `renderScale` to 0.5 and render every fourth frame to keep the backdrop alive.

**10 — Distant terrain goes flat and the horizon steps. All surface frames. Owner: Astra.**
`detailFade = 1 - smoothstep(120, 1600, range)` in `src/surface-materials.js` means everything past ~1.6 km is
vertex colour only — smooth plastic hills — until the albedo map fades in at 20–80 km, leaving a dead band in
between. Separately, every surface and lunar frame shows a stair-stepped band of rectangles along the horizon
where patch LODs meet, which §1 forbids ("no popping (geomorph + hysteresis)").
*Fix:* add a cheap two-octave macro-variation term that carries material from 1.6 km to ~20 km; geomorph patch
edge vertices toward the parent height over the last 15 % of the split distance, with hysteresis on the split
threshold so it cannot oscillate.

### Callout: there is a second, better build sitting unadopted

Six substantial modules are reachable only from `/dev/*` pages and are imported by nothing in the shipped graph:
`src/lighting-csm.js` (513 lines, cascaded shadows — the shipped `src/lighting.js` is 51 lines with a single
±110 m shadow box, which is why shadows stop at the LOD0/LOD1 tree boundary), `src/terrain-material.js` (326),
`src/ocean.js` (435 — merged as PR #13 "ocean distance fidelity", yet `src/planet.js` uses `water.js`),
`src/clouds.js` (264), `src/ship-mk2.js` (391) and `src/equipment.js` (1255). Roughly 3 000 lines of the better
version of exactly the things this report marks as whitebox. **Before commissioning new art, decide per module:
adopt it, or delete it.** Adopting `lighting-csm.js` and `ocean.js` alone would move defects 4, 3 and 10.

## 4. What is genuinely good — do not "fix" these away

* **The ship cabin (`12-ramp.png`).** Mint LED strips, dark polymer walls, amber floor safety lines, a red-trimmed
  cargo container, a legible corridor to the cockpit. This is the faction language working. Light it, don't
  redesign it.
* **The four cockpit MFDs (`src/ship-mfd.js`).** Numbered 01–04 pages, biggest-number-wins hierarchy, mint on
  dark. The best information design in the product. It only needs resolution and a bezel.
* **The flight HUD (`11-deck-firstperson.png`).** ALTITUDE numeral, ON FOOT / ASSIST secondary labels,
  coordinates, and the keycap legend row along the bottom. Clean and on-token.
* **The opening's staging at t ≈ 10 s.** Ship at frame left, pilot below centre, planet limb in the aperture,
  deck stripes leading the eye. Genuine cinematography — the composition is not the problem, the lighting is.
* **Orbit (`01-orbit.png`).** Continents, ice caps and atmospheric rim read instantly. The albedo worker and the
  1024² planet map are doing real work.
* **Highlands macro-relief (`r1-04-highlands.png`).** Layered ridges receding into aerial perspective. The
  heightfield is good; only its material and exposure let it down.
* **The station's silhouette.** Truss, hab spine, ring, dish, solar wings — reads as a station in one glance.
  It needs surfacing, not remodelling.
* **`src/ground-textures.js`.** Four physically-scaled layers with cellular gravel, fracture cracks, moss and
  ripple sand. Good material authoring that simply is not reaching the screen past 120 m or on snow.
* **The system map's chrome and accessibility.** Native `<dialog>`, `aria-pressed` on targets, focus return,
  ESC handling, correct phone reflow. Keep all of it; replace only the chart.
* **Zero console errors and zero warnings** across every run, and the whole build inside the §5 asset budgets.

## 5. Recommendation

**Not mergeable as a release candidate** (2.6 vs the 4.0 bar; six viewpoints score 1 on at least one criterion).
Suggested order: #1 and #2 first — they are shader/LOD-parameter changes with the largest visible payoff per line
— then #7 (the complaint of record), then #8, then the asset work in #5 and #6.
