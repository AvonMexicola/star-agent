## Remaining work and diagnostic closure

1. **Correct the review lighting while retaining cast/contact shadows.** Both original “shadow-off” PNGs still show cast shadows because toggling only `renderer.shadowMap.enabled` after compilation left cached shadow-receiving programs active. The corrected fixture disabled shadows before first draw, disabled the casting key and receivers, and asserted these states. I inspected its cockpit and rear frames: the diagonal bands disappear completely, including those on the petrol roof and dark dashboard; the floor cast shadow is correctly absent. This is fixture shadow acne, not proof of defective albedo or normals. Preserve the failed controls and use a properly biased shadow-on fixture for the next native score.
2. **Refine the few overly polished parts.** In native `03-cockpit.png` and the corrected control, the two cockpit control bases and lamp stalks produce soft bright blobs. Use the existing rougher brushed finish there while preserving machined edge breaks. Cutter-ring smoothing is already an improvement. The measurements do not justify another wholesale cabin, wheel or door redesign.
3. **Use the active carrier's inventory name.** `full-03/05-ore-bins.png` says “Nomad cargo” beside the correct “Rover mineral bin.” The parent’s presentation-layer fix preserves save/container IDs, which is the appropriate scope. The shown 1.13 kg basalt, 0.03 kg copper and 0.53 kg ice total about 1.69 kg; the later 0.69 kg follows the reported transfer and does not demonstrate lost ore.
4. **Keep the actual-game framing claims narrow.** `01-atlas-cargo-rover.png` faces a wall and is not an exterior fit view. `02-enclosed-cockpit.png` still says “CABIN ACCESS MOVING” and clips the lower MFD, so it is not a settled pilot-camera acceptance frame. `06-reloaded-atlas.png` is the valid cargo-fit image. The rear mining frame shows two distinct beams and their bright impact, but obscures the exact emitter roots; it alone cannot certify tip-origin mathematics.

The parent has narrowed the next candidate to rover-local cylindrical normals (radial sides and flat end caps, preserving vertices/triangles), a modest increase in steel-swatch roughness, and runtime transparent glazing that no longer casts solid shadows. These are proposed closures, not retroactively credited to the frozen 09 asset or original frames. The new portable native fixture preserves PBR materials and cast/contact shadows while correcting the light-frustum fit and receiver bias; its next render will establish whether acne is removed without visibly detaching contact shadows.
