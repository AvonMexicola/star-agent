# Settlement supplies and deliveries

Land on a settlement pad, leave the ship and walk to its exchange terminal.
**Local stock** shows the actual warehouse quantities, a purchase quote, the
remaining need and what the resource supports. **Cargo** sells a crate from the
landed ship. Credits, cargo and the destination warehouse change together.

| Settlement | Main exports | Imports and purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Greenbank Supply, Aeon | Aggregate, metal stock, conductors | Basalt and copper for construction inputs; water for crews and concrete |
| Stillwater Exchange, Selene | Ice, basalt, copper | Aggregate for habitats; metal and conductors for extraction equipment |
| Ember Works, Pyre | Metal stock, conductors | Ore feedstock, cooling water and foundation aggregate |
| Verdigris Prospect, Miasma | Copper, basalt | Clean water, habitat materials and replacement frames and wiring |

The map’s **Trade settlements** filter shows a short list of available exports
and current shortages. Select a settlement to inspect its quantities before
travelling. Deliveries reduce its needs; buying stock reduces its reserves.
The same finite-stock price curve used at station exchanges prices each unit,
so moving plentiful exports to a shortage can earn credits. Same-site buy/sell
round trips retain the spread.

A settlement buys only up to its reserve target. If a crate exceeds the remaining
need, it stays in your cargo and no credits change. Supply another market or
choose a smaller delivery. All local stock remains available to buy, including
import reserves. Export reserves also become replenishment needs when depleted.

Existing saves keep their current stock and balances; only missing settlement
markets receive the initial supply profile. Old reserves above their new target
show no need until stock falls below it. There is no automatic production,
consumption or refill timer. These exchanges currently belong to solo play;
player-owned shared base shops retain their separate stock and sale controls.

Use the standard controller’s Ship menu → Map for discovery, X to interact,
D-pad and A to choose terminal actions, and B to return to play. The local
launcher offers settlement approaches. [Validation](qa/settlement-stock-needs/README.md)
records the tested route and remaining hardware acceptance.
