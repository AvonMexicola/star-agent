# Transport missions

Open **Menu → Contracts → Transport contracts**, or **Trade → Freight**.
Choose a cargo ship and accept one route. Acceptance records your contract;
it does not put a crate at either settlement or in your ship.

1. Fly to the pickup settlement. Track it through **Map → Locations**, land on
   its pad and walk to the trade terminal.
2. Open **Freight → Order my crate**. Your sealed 1 SBU crate appears on the
   loading apron. Only the player who accepted this contract can see or handle
   that crate. Ordering again cannot issue another.
3. Equip the tractor beam, guide the crate through the cargo hatch and secure
   it on your ship's cargo grid. A loose or handheld crate is not ready for
   interplanetary departure.
4. Fly to the destination, land your loaded ship on its pad and walk to its
   terminal. **Freight → Deposit crate** removes the original sealed crate and
   pays the displayed reward. You do not need to unload it onto the ground.

The four stops are Greenbank Supply on Aeon, Stillwater Exchange on Selene,
Ember Works on Pyre and Verdigris Prospect on Miasma. Twelve directional routes
connect them. A trip within either planet/moon group pays 350 CR; a trip between
the groups pays 800 CR. These are sealed contract supplies, separate from the
settlements' ordinary commodity stock and material needs.

Use Nomad, Atlas, Stratum or Gannet; Kestrel has no cargo grid. Your selected
cargo ship must actually be parked on the correct pad when ordering or depositing.
Only the exact crate issued to your current contract is accepted. Ordinary
crates cannot substitute for it, and sealed freight cannot be sold or deposited
as ordinary stock. Keep other crates off it if you want to deposit or abandon
the contract without rearranging the cargo stack.

With a standard controller, Menu opens the command screen, D-pad moves focus,
A selects and B returns. At the terminal, X interacts. The equipped tractor
uses held RT to guide, D-pad up/down to adjust distance and X to secure at a
valid grid slot. Keyboard equivalents are Escape, Tab/Enter, F, held T and
the beam's displayed distance controls. The terminal also has touch buttons.
The regular flight and controller bindings remain visible in the control guide.

For interplanetary travel, close up, launch, retract the gear and climb above
20 km. Select the settlement marker, point at it until charged and engage the
drive (controller LB + RB + D-pad up). A route through a planet is blocked;
fly around its limb first. Arrival is outside the settlement and requires
normal flight and landing. Online targeted drive is available for your active
contract's pickup and delivery; other targeted routes retain their existing
availability. Free-heading drive remains available.

One contract can be active at a time. There is no deadline or abandonment fee.
**Abandon contract** recalls your sealed crate and pays nothing. Reopening a
save or reconnecting preserves your contract and original crate; it does not
grant replacement cargo. Solo saves and signed-in online accounts use separate
inventories. Completion and payment are committed together and cannot be
collected twice by repeating a terminal request.
