# Main power and walking during flight

**P** switches main power on or off from the pilot chair. The same control is in
**H → MAIN POWER**. With a standard controller, open **Menu / Options**, then
press **A / Cross** on the focused power button. D-pad selects menu controls;
A activates, B / Circle closes, and the right stick scrolls. Touch users can tap
the power button in the menu. Release held controller inputs before resuming.

**F** (controller **X / Square**) leaves the seat during ordinary flight. Walk
through the Nomad cabin or Atlas cargo deck using the existing movement controls,
then approach the chair and press F to resume piloting. Seat occupancy and ship
power are independent.

While unseated with main power and flight assist on, the ship holds its captured
world heading and velocity. Walking and looking move the passenger inside the
ship; they do not steer or brake the hull. This is course hold, not obstacle
avoidance or an autopilot to a destination. Existing collision and touchdown
handling still run. On reseating, normal assisted controls resume, so releasing
thrust brakes as before. In inertial flight, existing gravity, drag and rotation
continue while the pilot walks.

Main power off disables thrust, boost, attitude control, braking, launch,
landing assist, cargo lifts and travel-drive engagement. Shutdown preserves
momentum and lets gravity and aerodynamic forces act. It cancels landing or
undocking assistance; the relativistic drive must disengage before shutdown.
MFDs report power loss and propulsion audio stops. Emergency cabin displays,
lighting, storage and parked hatch access remain available.

External hatches and Atlas's belly elevator stay secured during flight. Atlas's
internal cargo lifts work while powered, including while cruising. Finish landing,
undocking or relativistic travel before leaving the chair. Moving-ship EVA,
automatic obstacle avoidance, power distribution and battery depletion are outside
this change. Menus retain the game's existing simulation pause.
