# System travel

Press **M** or click **SYSTEM** to open the map. Select **Aeon** or **Selene**,
then click **ENGAGE DRIVE**. Selecting a world only sets a course. You can also
close the map and press **J** to engage the selected route.

The drive aligns the ship, spools for three seconds, accelerates along a checked
straight path, then brakes automatically above the destination. A cyan/violet
tunnel appears during spool-up and travel. **X** or **J** aborts with controlled
braking; controller B / circle also aborts. Ordinary steering, landing and
boarding are locked during travel. After arrival, normal flight resumes:
descend with W or C and use L for landing below 12 km.

Opening the map pauses flight, including an active drive. M or Escape returns
to flight. The selected target persists. Existing destination shortcuts remain
explicit optional teleports.

## Speeds and clearance

| Mode | Cruise | Shift boost |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Aeon atmosphere | 250 m/s | 400 m/s |
| Space | 3,000 m/s | 9,000 m/s |
| Inter-body drive | Up to 0.9c | Automatic |

Manual speed rises smoothly between 20 and 70 km above Aeon's reference radius.
On airless Selene, the transition spans 2–20 km. Terrain proximity and station
approaches lower commanded speeds. Mouse wheel / controller D-pad adjusts
throttle from 5% to 100%; it does not multiply the maximum speed.

Drive entry requires clearance above Aeon's 100 km or Selene's 20 km exclusion
zone. The entire route must clear both bodies and a 2 km safety sphere around
Aeon Orbital. A blocked map route explains which exclusion zone is involved:
climb or reposition in ordinary flight, then try again. There is no automatic
route around an obstruction.

Arrival stops 150 km above Aeon's reference radius or 50 km above Selene's.
Actual terrain-relative altitude differs with terrain height. Terrain, collision
and the planet seed remain governed by the existing world generators.

**0.9c is a maximum, not a fixed speed.** At the current 24,000 km moon distance,
acceleration and braking keep the peak near 0.1c, depending on the departure point.
A typical journey takes about five seconds including spool-up and drive exit.
Longer routes can reach 0.9c. This is a gameplay drive; it does not simulate
time dilation, relativistic energy or realistic human acceleration tolerance.

The schematic map contains the two implemented explorable worlds. The star is
context only. It does not promise additional planets or station drive destinations.
