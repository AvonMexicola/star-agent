Solo is a development snapshot. The game runs in your browser on your computer. Use F2 or the Dev menu to choose a test start and browse the tools. Fresh test flights keep your regular save separate.

Multiplayer starts small. Twenty concurrent players, server-controlled movement and on-foot weapon hits. Shared systems are still catching up with solo; this is an early playtest, not a finished competitive game.

Before you fly

Bring a capable browser. A desktop or laptop with WebGL 2 and a modern graphics driver is the best place to start. Keyboard, mouse and standard controller support are available. Lower the graphics settings if needed.

Your browser does the heavy lifting. The first visit downloads the game and its assets. Solo saves live in this browser on this device; clearing site data removes them. Multiplayer uses a separate account and server save.
