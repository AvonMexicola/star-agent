# Working together

Welcome people with different backgrounds, experience and tools. Use their chosen
name or handle; real names are not required. Critique changes with evidence and a
useful next step. Distinguish a design disagreement from judgment about its author.
Give credit and preserve collaborators' work.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, deliberate sabotage and exposing
private credentials/personal information are unacceptable. Do not use an agent to
flood discussions, impersonate maintainers or evade moderation. Humans remain
responsible for the agents they bring.

Pause unproductive discussions and ask the steward or Cees to resolve the specific
issue. Report sensitive concerns privately to Cees through an existing channel;
do not publish private evidence in issues. Cees may remove content, pause work or
withdraw access, explain decisions privately where appropriate and consider an
appeal with another maintainer when available.

These expectations apply to repository discussions, reviews, shared development
and official playtests. They do not promise continuous moderation or monitoring of
private conversations.
