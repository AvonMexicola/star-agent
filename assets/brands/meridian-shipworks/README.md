# Meridian Shipworks

Cees approved this shared manufacturer for Nomad, Kestrel and Atlas on 2026-09-07.
The compass axis and forward chevron form an M through negative space. Use the
single pale-mint mark on dark hulls and screens; retain generous clear space.

`emblem.svg` is the production master, authored as filled vector paths.
`emblem.webp` is its 512 px transparent decal raster. The fleet wordmark uses
real type alongside the symbol. Identity values live in `identity.json`.

`concept.png` is the original imagegen concept, generated in this session. Its
alpha edges had unwanted pale fringe. Two imagegen cleanup passes were rejected
(one baked in a checkerboard; the other retained fringe). The production SVG
was redrawn as new vector geometry and rasterized with ImageMagick, rather than
shipping those artifacts. No external trademark or downloaded logo was used.

Original generation prompt:

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production manufacturer emblem for an original browser spaceflight game, to appear as a small hull decal, machined badge, cockpit boot mark and fleet-menu icon. Primary request: design a cool, distinctive emblem for MERIDIAN SHIPWORKS, the common manufacturer of a compact utility tender, a sleek interceptor and a heavy cargo hauler. Create the SYMBOL ONLY; no words, letters, numbers, slogan, borders or presentation mockup. Visual idea: a precise angular meridian/navigation instrument combined with a confident forward flight chevron, subtly suggesting an M through negative space. One cohesive compact mark, strong silhouette and purposeful asymmetric cut or offset, engineered rather than ornamental. Style: refined flat graphic, vector-like optical precision, aerospace industrial identity, timeless and credible; suitable for stencil printing and embossed metal. Palette: one solid very pale mint #b6efd1 only. Transparent background with genuine alpha, clean antialiased edges, no white backing. Center a single emblem, fill roughly 75% of the square canvas, generous even clear space. Keep the design legible at 24 px, with broad continuous shapes and at most 4–5 simple components. No fine filigree, generic swoosh, wings, shield, star field, gradients, glows, shadows, texture, bevel shading, watermarks, NASA/Drake/Star Citizen or other existing logos.
